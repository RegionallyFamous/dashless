# Department of Small Problems

A cheerful municipal publication applying disproportionate seriousness to tiny everyday problems.

Feeling: dry, sunny, tactile, nosy, reassuring.
Palette revised after customer review: warm off-white #f8f7f4, white panels, charcoal #282727, slate blue #334f68, and rust #984b38. Notes use a neutral sand #eee9df; the demo banner is quiet stone #eae7e1. Night palette: charcoal #202224, soft white #f1efea, pale slate #b1c8da, muted copper #e8a68f. The photograph supplies the warmth; the interface stays neutral.
Typography: Georgia for big expressive headlines and reading; Arial for navigation; monospace for dates and case numbers.
Signature: numbered evidence slips and a slightly crooked red department stamp. Quiet area: generous, unboxed article prose.

This is an explicitly commissioned fictional customer demonstration, not production customer content. All writing is original sample copy. The generated evidence photograph is illustrative and openly labeled. No content was written to WordPress.
