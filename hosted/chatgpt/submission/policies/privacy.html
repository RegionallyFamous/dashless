Information we process

We process your Auth0 account ID, verified email address and display name to identify your account; blog settings, drafts, revisions, published posts, images, alternative text and design choices to provide your blog; subscription and invoice references and payment status to administer access; and job, authorization, security and support records to operate and troubleshoot the service. Requests can include technical information such as an IP address, browser information and timestamps. Please do not put passwords, payment-card details or other unnecessary sensitive information in blog content or support messages.

How information is used and shared

We use this information to authenticate you, perform the editorial actions you request, build and host your site, administer your subscription, answer support requests, prevent abuse and meet applicable legal obligations. Auth0 handles sign-in and authorizes the ChatGPT connection. Dashless validates those credentials but does not store your password or provider access and refresh tokens. A secure browser cookie keeps you signed in.

WP Cloud hosts the account service and blog data. Railway processes copies of content, media and design settings to build your site. Cloudflare provides domain, network and email-routing services. Stripe processes payments when billing is available; Dashless retains payment references rather than full payment-card numbers. Our email providers process support and service messages. These providers may process information in the United States and other countries.

When you connect ChatGPT, the tools return content, settings, theme choices and operation results needed for your requested actions to OpenAI. Images you select in ChatGPT can be transferred to your blog. OpenAI’s own terms, privacy controls and data practices govern your use of ChatGPT. Disconnecting Dashless does not erase an existing ChatGPT conversation. Publishing makes the approved content and included media publicly accessible; readers, search engines and other third parties may copy it.

Cookies and access

Dashless uses essential cookies for sign-in, account sessions, private previews and protection against forged requests. Private preview access requires authorization. Publication and rollback require approval in your signed-in browser. You can disconnect ChatGPT from your Dashless account to revoke its existing access and refresh-token chains.

Retention

We keep blog content and account records while providing the service. After paid access ends, the service provides a 30-day recovery and export period before the site becomes eligible for deletion. Deletion is processed after checking current subscription status; it is not an instantaneous erasure of every provider backup or record. A reserved blog name may remain unavailable after deletion to prevent old links from being reassigned to another person.

Railway build data is scheduled for cleanup after 24 hours. Private preview access expires after 24 hours; this access deadline is not a promise that stored preview or export artifacts are erased at that moment. Those artifacts can remain with the site until removed during maintenance or site deletion. Hub audit records are scheduled to expire after 90 days. Short-lived authorization and security records expire according to their purpose and are removed by maintenance.

Account, billing, job and support records, and hosting-provider backups, may be retained separately where needed to provide recovery, resolve a request or dispute, prevent fraud, or meet legal obligations. Contact us to request deletion or to ask which records remain and why. We verify ownership before acting on requests and explain any applicable retention requirement or technical limitation.
