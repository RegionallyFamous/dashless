# A little less obedient

The user requested more creative marks after rejecting familiar writing/publishing pictograms. Three new exploratory directions:

- Loose Leaf: a printed post becomes a bird. Strong silhouette, but potential social-media associations and subtle page reading.
- Big Mouth: expressive typographic a with an open mouth and one detached stroke. Related to Hot Type, but an isolated a is not automatically a Dashless monogram and the stroke may read as a dash.
- Post Punk: torn zine scrap with three negative-space text lines. Keeps the writing content visible with a less orderly exterior; silhouette can read as a flag/burst.

These are concept alternatives rather than completed identities. No readiness claim for favicon, vector master or print production. Generated through built-in imagegen, with a second pass correcting dark contrast artifacts. Original prompts saved in generation-prompts.txt; both versions retained.
