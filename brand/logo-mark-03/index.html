Dashless / Mark explorations / A little less obedient

Give the mark
something to say.

Three less literal ideas with stronger personalities. The question is which feels like Dashless before its explanation does any work.

01 / Loose Leaf

A post takes flight. Its writing becomes the wing. More expressive than a document, with a sense of leaving the draft behind.

The bird is clear; the page is subtler. Needs distance from familiar social-media bird silhouettes.

02 / Big Mouth

A letter with an opinion. The counter opens into a mouth, giving the wordmark’s chunky typography a mischievous little relative.

The strongest relationship to the lettering. The isolated “a” and detached stroke need further exploration; neither is an automatic fit for the name.

03 / Post Punk

A piece of writing that refuses to sit quietly in a rectangle. A torn zine scrap with a bold, uneven silhouette.

Keeps the writing visible and adds rebellion. The silhouette can also read as a flag or burst, which needs testing.

Creative studies, not final logo assets. These have not been tested as favicons or print artwork.
