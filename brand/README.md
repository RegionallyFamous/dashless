## Direction

Rebellious, playful blogging software. The Rip represents a piece of writing with a torn edge. Keep the tilted compact silhouette, three unequal writing lines, and substantial tear shapes. Pair its angular edges with the softer, expressive Hot Type lettering. Ink, Paper, and Acid remain the primary palette.

## Remaining production work

The Rip is a vector sketch. Refine its contours and spacing; draw a dedicated 16px favicon. Hot Type remains a raster wordmark concept and needs a consistent vector master. Final combination exports and physical print proofs have not been completed.

All previous studies are retained as exploration history. Selection does not apply artwork to the released product or publisher sites.
