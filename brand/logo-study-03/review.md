# Dashless — Hot Type

The user liked the rebellious identity but found the previous upright sans logo boring. This direction moves toward a characterful independent publication masthead: substantial soft slab serifs, sloped ascender tops, a tilted e, and bulbous repeating s terminals. The word carries the identity without requiring a symbol or mascot.
