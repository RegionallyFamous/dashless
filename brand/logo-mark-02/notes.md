# Blogging mark explorations

The user rejected the bear mark because the blogging connection was not clear. These are three new routes, not further bear variants.

1. Byline: fountain pen nib and ink drop. Clear authorship, but familiar writing-brand territory.
2. The Post: a chunky page with headline and text bars. Clear publishing object, but generic document associations.
3. Out Loud: opening quotation mark plus broadcast arcs. Combines writing and distribution, with an RSS/blogging connection. Recommended for further exploration, while checking podcast/wireless ambiguity.

The selection remains open. These generated symbols are exploratory concepts, not final logo assets. Small-size performance, vector construction and print reproduction have not been tested.

Built-in imagegen created the exploration sheet. Initial output had dark lighting artifacts; v2 corrects contrast. The full prompts are saved in generation-prompts.txt. Earlier logo work is preserved.
