# Dashless logo study 02 — Marge review

## Initial logo

The compressed, heavily slanted wordmark conveys speed but offers little distinctive identity for independent publishing. Small counters, crowded letters, and the repeated narrow s forms compromise small-size clarity. Keep the palette and supporting bear; replace the lettering.

## New direction

An upright, moderately wide lowercase wordmark with open counters and one distinctive interruption in the d. The new lettering reads more clearly and works without the campaign palette or mascot.

## Focused revision

The first open d had a square shelf projecting into the opening, creating a power-symbol or musical-note association. The revised artwork replaces that shelf with a curved bowl and a single clean break near its upper-left corner. The same revision appears in the wordmark and standalone mark.

Selected artwork: `open-frame-v3.png`.

## Screen proofs

`size-proof.png` records a browser screenshot at device scale 1, showing the wordmark at 240, 160, and 120 CSS pixels wide and the icon tile at 16, 24, 32, and 64 CSS pixels. The preview uses CSS viewports of the generated raster, not independently redrawn logo variants. These are screen estimates, not print proofs.

## Status

Creative concept, not production master. Vector drawing, optical spacing, transparent one-color exports, and dedicated small-size icon drawing remain production work. The original identity guide is preserved; its logo has not been silently replaced with an unselected direction.

## Generation

Built-in imagegen generated the study and made the revisions. Full prompts are in `generation-prompts.txt`. The first rendering had lighting artifacts and is retained as `open-frame-v1.png` for provenance; v2 corrected the presentation; v3 corrects the d. The presentation is a raster concept, not a source font or SVG master.

## Final independent concept verdict

Keep this direction. The corrected d reads as a letter with a deliberate opening, and the awkward tab is gone. The upright wordmark has enough weight to preserve the rebellious character.

The name is readable at the shown 240, 160, and 120px widths. The cut is less prominent at 120px, but the name remains clear. The isolated mark is clear at 32 and 64px.

The 16px icon needs its own drawing: enlarge the letter within the square and optically widen the cut for the 16/24px exports. At 16px, the current raster opening can resemble a stray light pixel. Approval is for the replacement concept only. Final vector paths, optical refinement, small icon exports, and a physical sticker proof remain unverified.
