# Dashless logo study 02

The user likes the rebellious, playful, anti-dashboard identity and rejects the initial logo. Rework the logo while retaining the Ink/Paper/Acid palette and the independent publishing positioning. The bear remains a supporting character.

Primary line: WordPress without the dashboard.

Required uses: web masthead, repository identity, stickers, reversed one-color application, and a separate small favicon treatment.

Study goal: recognizable lettering with an ownable idea, readable at modest size. Explore the logo in black and white before using the palette. This is an art-direction study, not a claim of trademark distinctiveness.
