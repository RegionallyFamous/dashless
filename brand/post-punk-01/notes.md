The user selected Post Punk. This iteration preserves the torn piece of writing and tests three ways to shape its rough edge.

- The Clipping: straight edges and a torn bottom-right corner. Restrained, closest to a document.
- The Rip: tilted compact scrap with a broken right edge. Recommended development: recognizable writing plus an asymmetric tear.

The SVG sketch keeps substantial turns on the right edge and removes fine texture. These are application sketches rather than exact automatic traces of the generated study.
